🛠️ Maintenance
Agar kisi purani Cashbook entry ka date/amount Paid Account ledger mein alag dikh raha ho (jab date change karne par link wale side update nahi hota tha), ye button ek dafa chala kar sab ko wapas match kar dein.
Agar aapke paas ek purana JSON backup hai jisme Paid No sahi thay (kisi galat "fix" se pehle ka), ye us backup ki file select karke sirf har entry ka Paid No field wapas ussi se restore kar dega — ID se match karke. Baaki kuch (date/amount/naam) ya koi bhi nayi entry nahi chhuyi jayegi.
Cashbook, Party & Paid Entry, aur Opening Balance ki har entry ka Paid Account link ek hi click mein dobara refresh/recompute kar dega — kisi ek-ek row ko khol kar dobara tap karne ki zaroorat nahi.
Agar Baqaya/Jama mein thoda sa farq aa raha ho (jaise 1 rupee), to ye wo ledger entries dhoondta hai jin ki Party & Paid / Opening Balance row delete ho chuki hai, aur aap ki tasdeeq ke baad unhein hata deta hai.
